Experimental in-season formula

How teams are evaluated

50% · Schedule strength

Quality of opponents played, based on their earned ratings.

25% · Result quality

Credit for actual game outcomes against rated opponents.

15% · Winning percentage

Season record matters; victories are earned, not projected.

10% · Conference strength

League strength is derived from results, not conference branding.

No margin-of-victory bonus. No subjective committee overrides. Experimental v0.5 · Prior v0.4 weights: 40/30/20/10. The new weights improved winner predictions on a reserved 2025 holdout; results remain experimental.
